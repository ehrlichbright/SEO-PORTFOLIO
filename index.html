Background

This is Wellington College's first campus in Africa. The group also has schools in the UK, China, Thailand and India. The Lagos school opens in Alaro City, Lekki, in September 2027, with places from Early Years to A-Levels. It needs to rank in search results and appear in AI answers before it opens. Two competitors, Charterhouse Lagos and Rugby School Nigeria, opened first and already have stronger rankings.

Technical SEO audit

I crawled all 34 pages of the live site with Screaming Frog. I sorted the findings by severity so the client's web developers could fix the most serious issues first.

PriorityIssueEffect
CriticalTwo live pages had no meta description, including one used by an ad campaignLower click-through rate from Google
CriticalThree broken redirect chains ending in 404 errors, including one caused by a typo in an internal linkLost link equity; dead ends for users and crawlers
Important27 of 32 page titles were longer than about 60 charactersTitles cut off in search results
ImportantTwo H1 tags on every page; no H2 tags on the homepage and one key pageWeak heading structure for search engines and AI tools
ImportantTwo uncompressed images (14MB and 9.6MB)Slower page loads
MonitorNo structured data on any pageLikely reason AI tools showed the wrong opening date

Result so far: the web developers have added the missing meta descriptions and shortened most of the long titles. The remaining titles and the schema markup are in progress.

Schema markup

The school's opening date was inconsistent across its own pages. A parent-group website, which AI tools were using as a source, also listed the wrong date. I wrote a JSON-LD specification using EducationalOrganization, Course and FAQPage types. This gives search engines and AI tools one clear, machine-readable source for the correct date.

AI search visibility

Many parents now ask AI tools questions such as "What are the best British schools in Lagos?" I set up a monthly test to check whether and how the school appears in those answers.

  • 40 prompts in five groups: local searches, boarding, branded, competitor comparisons and diaspora relocation.
  • Tested separately on ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Copilot, because each tool uses different sources.
  • Scored on mention rate, position-weighted share of voice and an overall visibility score. The method is documented, so the client can see how each score is calculated.
  • First findings: AI tools were mixing up the Lagos school with the UK school, and one tool gave the wrong opening year. Both issues have been flagged for correction.
